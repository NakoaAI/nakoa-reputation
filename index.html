FAQ

Common questions.

Does it actually post responses automatically?
On the Growth and Pro plans you can choose: either approve each draft with one click, or turn on auto-post and it publishes automatically. You're always in control.
How does it sound like my business?
During setup you choose your tone and enter details about your business. Every AI draft is generated with that context baked in — the more you use it, the better it learns your voice.
Which review platforms are supported?
Google Business Profile, Yelp, and Facebook on Starter and Growth. TripAdvisor is added on the Pro plan. More platforms coming soon.
What happens after the 14-day trial?
You'll be prompted to enter a payment method. If you don't, your account pauses — no charges, no drama. No credit card is required to start.
Can I manage multiple locations?
Yes. Growth supports up to 3 locations and Pro supports up to 10. Each location gets its own monitoring, alerts, and response settings.
Is there a setup fee or contract?
No setup fee, no contracts, no commitments. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime from your dashboard in under 30 seconds.
